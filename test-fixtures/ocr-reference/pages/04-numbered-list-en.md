Mourning routine before a long walk

1. Check the forecast for the whole day, not just the morning.
2. Pack the bag in this order:
   a. water first, at the bottom
   b. food and the map in the middle
   c. jacket on top, easy to reach
3. Leave a note saying which route you took.
4. Start earlier than feels necessary.
