## Short answers to the two explicit questions

- **Sub-pages: yes.** `type: 'page'` is a navigable entry that slides in a sub-page with a back
  button. It can be declarative (`items`) or imperative (`page: () => SettingPage`), and pages can
  nest (d.ts 6202–6259, 6461–6508; Settings 623–719). Since 1.13.1 an entry can show
  `displayValue` and `status: 'warning'` (d.ts 6227, 6235).
  **Tabs: no.** There is no tab strip inside one plugin's tab. **Collapsible groups: no.**
  `SettingDefinitionGroup` has `type`, `heading`, `cls`, `search`, `extraButtons`, `items` and
  `visible`, and no collapse field (d.ts 6079–6140). Neither doc mentions collapsing. (The 1.14.0
  changelog line "Groups are now collapsible" is about Bases views, not settings.) **In-tab
  navigation:** only pages, plus Obsidian's global settings search, which jumps to a result. There
  is no anchor, table of contents or "jump to group". Our two `<details>` folds (pairing help,
  *Advanced* under the localhost backend) can only survive as native HTML inside a `render` row.
- **A list control for user-added items: yes, `type: 'list'`** (d.ts 6157–6194; Settings 514–621).
  It adds a denser style, `onDelete` (a delete button plus the Delete/Backspace key), `onReorder`
  (drag handle; Alt-↑/↓ since CL 1.13.1), `emptyState`, and `addItem` (a `+` button in the header
  on desktop). Each item is an ordinary definition (`control`, `render`, `action` or empty). The list
  is a vertical list of rows, not chips, so tag pills are not a built-in layout. A group or list can
  also carry an in-header search box that filters its direct children (`search.match`, d.ts
  6104–6119, `@since 1.13.1`).

## What pays off most (ranked)

1. **`visible`/`disabled` + `refreshDomState()` + auto-refresh after controls** (1.13.0). This
   removes the full-tab redraw behind most of the 18 `redraw()` calls and the scroll-jump workaround,
   and it states our rule "shown disabled, not hidden" in the API's own terms.
2. **Sub-pages with `displayValue`/`status`** (1.13.1). Zotero (6 headings) and Readwise leave the
   main tab as two entries that still show their state and flag a problem. This is the biggest cut in
   tab length.
3. **List + group `search` + `extraButtons` for Tag routing** (1.13.1). Mapped tags become a
   *tag → folder* list with delete and `+`, with a filter box for 20–50 tags and the scan as a header
   icon. This covers ground rule 4 except the pills.
4. **Controls with an overridden `get/setControlValue`** (1.13.0). About 12 plain rows lose their
   hand-written `onChange`/save/debounce code, and the side effects move into one switch per key. The
   `folder` control gives *Attachments folder* a vault suggester.
5. **Global settings search + `aliases`** (1.13.0). This is the user-visible gain the store's
   `prefer-setting-definitions` Warning points at. It is worth most for credentials and paths people
   look for by word.
6. **`secret` control** (1.13.2) for the Readwise token. Small, but it removes our only direct
   `SecretComponent` use.

## Custom pieces that must stay `render` (or an imperative page), and what they lose

All of them lose auto-save (each `onChange` keeps its own `saveData`) and automatic predicate
refresh (they call `refreshDomState()`). Search sees only the definition's own `name`/`desc`/`aliases`.
